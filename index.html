Before you download

A quick license check can save you trouble.

“Free to download” does not always mean “free for every use.” A platform can host works with different creators, permissions, and restrictions. Check the license on the individual asset page—not only the site's headline or search result.

  1. Open the original item page. Confirm the creator, source URL, and exact license attached to that file.
  2. Match the license to your use. Look for rules about commercial use, modification, redistribution, attribution, and sharing adaptations.
  3. Keep a record. Save the download date, item URL, creator name, license version, and any required credit with your project.
  4. Check people, brands, and models separately. An asset license may not include a model release, trademark permission, or rights to third-party material in the image.

Common license signals

  • CC0: designed for broad reuse, subject to rights that may not be covered by the dedication.
  • CC BY: reuse is allowed when you give the required attribution and follow the license terms.
  • MIT / Apache: software licenses; keep required notices and check any patent or attribution terms.
  • OFL: designed for font software; font redistribution and embedding have specific conditions.
  • Custom terms: read the actual terms. “Free” or “open” in a title is not a license grant.
