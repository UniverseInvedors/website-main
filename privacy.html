Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy describes the information practices for Universe Invedors Open Library at universeinvedors.tech. The site currently provides a static resource directory, informational pages, browser-side search and filters, and an attribution-note builder. It does not currently offer user accounts, file uploads, or an AI generation service.

Information handled by this site

Searching, filtering, and creating attribution drafts happen in your browser; this information is not submitted to a site search service or stored by the site. The copy button uses your browser clipboard only after you select it. The site does not provide a contact form or ask you to submit personal information. The hosting and delivery provider may process technical request information such as IP address, browser details, and requested page to deliver and protect the site.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

This site does not currently set advertising or analytics cookies or load third-party analytics scripts. If advertising or analytics are introduced, this policy and any required consent controls will be updated before they are enabled. The site does not currently serve Google AdSense ads.

External websites and services

Directory links take you to third-party websites. Those providers may collect information, use cookies, or process data under their own privacy policies and terms. Review their notices before using their services. This policy does not cover third-party sites, downloadable assets, AI platforms, or tools linked from this directory.

Data retention and your choices

We do not operate an account or form-submission system on this site. Technical records held by the hosting provider are governed by that provider's policies and retention practices. You can avoid following external links and can control cookies through your browser settings.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when site features or service providers change. The updated date at the top will be revised when the policy changes. For privacy questions, use the Universe Invedors contact page.

This policy is a plain-language description of the current static site and is not legal advice. Review it against the actual hosting, analytics, advertising, and legal requirements for your operation before launch.
